// 01 · about

About Me

I am a Data Scientist, AI Engineer and Machine Learning Engineer with an MSc in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in Sciences from Queen Mary University of London, following a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from PES University, India. I am driven by one simple goal: using AI to solve real problems, especially in healthcare, where good models can make a real difference to patients and clinicians.

As a Research Assistant in cardiac AI at the Digital Environment Research Institute (DERI), Queen Mary, I built AortaClip, a fully automated graph neural network pipeline that predicts aortic clipping planes from 3D CT meshes for patient-specific TAVI planning. It replaces slow manual work, reaches 1.48 mm mean surface distance and 0.88 Dice against expert results, and was accepted at MICAD 2026, where I am the first author. I also built reproducible workflows with Nextflow and Snakemake and trained models on multi-terabyte data using the Apocrita HPC cluster.

Before my MSc, I worked as an Associate Data Scientist at Happiest Minds Technologies. I developed 3D medical image segmentation models on clinical MRI that outperformed MONAI baselines, partnered with Quadram Institute Bioscience (UK) on antimicrobial resistance research, and built genomics data pipelines and multimodal models for drug synergy discovery. I also delivered end-to-end AI products, including a real-time recommendation system on Azure, a multilingual medical translation app using Llama 2 on Google Cloud, and a bone health image classifier.

Alongside this, I have led and built my own projects, from MedSense, an AI symptom checker where I owned the product roadmap, to ECG-based biological age prediction and electricity demand forecasting. I enjoy the full journey: understanding the problem, cleaning messy data, building and testing models, and turning results into something people can actually use.

I believe in doing the work properly, owning my results and always learning. Whether I am building a model, working with clinicians or presenting research, I aim to deliver work that is clear, reliable and useful.
